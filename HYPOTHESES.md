# Hypotheses

## H1 — prospective information

At least one population-level observable may contain predictive information about future cascade onset beyond the current unsafe fraction alone.

## H2 — transfer is not guaranteed

Predictive performance discovered in simplified dynamics may degrade under dynamic topology and heterogeneous susceptibility.

## H3 — boundary conditions are scientifically valuable

Failure to transfer is a valid outcome. The research goal is to measure predictive value and its validity boundary, not to prove a universal invariant.
