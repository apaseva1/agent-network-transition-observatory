# Falsification Contract

The funded early-warning hypothesis is weakened if candidate population observables:

- fail to outperform simple reactive/history baselines;
- provide negligible warning lead time;
- generate an unacceptable false-alarm burden;
- lose predictive advantage across model families, tasks, topologies, or protocols;
- invert under realistic adaptive agent dynamics.

A null or negative transfer result is scientifically interpretable and should be reported rather than hidden.
